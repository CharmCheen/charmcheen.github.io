About

I am a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science at Fudan University, affiliated with DASLab. Prior to joining Fudan, I completed my B.S. in Software Engineering at Wuhan University of Technology.

My broad interests lie in Database Systems and AI/Data Infrastructure. I am particularly interested in the execution layer of modern data-processing systems—including physical operator execution, data organization, scheduling, placement, and adaptive optimization. I am drawn to understanding how traditional database systems abstractions interact with and can be redesigned for large-scale, heterogeneous AI workloads.

Currently

I am currently exploring data infrastructure for large-scale AI workloads, especially how modern data-processing workloads challenge the assumptions behind physical execution and optimization.
